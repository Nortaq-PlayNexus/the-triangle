# Dreamcore Horror

*Draft page — proposed branch, not an established genre.*

## Definition

Dreamcore Horror is a proposed category for interactive works that:

1. meet the base Dreamcore provenance/materiality criteria;
2. are materially shaped by the creator's own frightening dreams, nightmares, or night terrors; and
3. are intended to produce horror or a related fear response.

## What it can include

- Psychological horror.
- Supernatural horror grounded in a personal nightmare.
- Slow dread, uncanny spaces, and horror without combat.
- Symbolic transformations of a recurring nightmare.
- Games that turn a dream into a puzzle, narrative, or rule system.

## What it does not mean

A dark palette, a dreamlike level, a monster called a nightmare, or a store tag is not enough. The creator's own frightening source and the material connection must be documented at a level the creator is comfortable sharing.

## THE TRIANGLE

THE TRIANGLE is currently a provisional example. Its repository implements lucidity, recognition, false awakenings, recurring geometric manifestations, a wrong train, and a dream-horror atmosphere. The creator's personal night-terror basis is reported in the project brief but is not yet a public provenance record.

## Privacy

A creator can say “based on a recurring nightmare” without publishing the dream. A directory should accept abstracted, consented descriptions and allow withdrawal.
