# Genre Specification

*Draft public presentation of the proposal. The canonical draft is [`GENRE/SPECIFICATION_v1.0.md`](../GENRE/SPECIFICATION_v1.0.md).*

## Minimum test

A work qualifies as Dreamcore when it is interactive, is materially influenced by a documented dream of a credited creator, and provides enough evidence to review the relationship.

Dreamcore Horror adds a documented frightening personal source and horror intention.

## Current hierarchy

```text
Dreamcore
└── Dreamcore Horror
```

Adventure, exploration, and narrative remain ordinary descriptors until a separate evidence case justifies new branches.

## Review outcomes

`confirmed`, `probable`, `candidate`, `adjacent`, `not_classifiable`, and `withdrawn` are used instead of inflated certainty.
