# FAQ — Draft Page

The canonical FAQ is [`GENRE/FAQ.md`](../GENRE/FAQ.md).

## Is this an official genre?

No. It is a proposed category being researched and documented.

## Can I use the word if I make a dreamlike game?

You may describe a game as dreamlike, surreal, liminal, or inspired by dreams. That does not automatically make it Dreamcore under the proposed definition. If you use the term, explain the personal-source and materiality relationship.

## Do I have to reveal my dream?

No. Abstract descriptions and consented evidence are sufficient.

## Does a badge certify quality or safety?

No. It would communicate an optional classification and review status only.

## Can I remove a listing?

Yes. A future directory must have a correction and withdrawal process.

## Why are earlier games included?

They demonstrate that the practice has precedents and help prevent false novelty claims. They are not being claimed as adopters.
