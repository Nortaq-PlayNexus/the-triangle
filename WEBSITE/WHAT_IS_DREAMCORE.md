# What Is Dreamcore?

*Draft public article — proposed category, not an established industry standard.*

## The short answer

Under this proposal, **Dreamcore** describes games substantially derived from a creator's own dream experiences.

The source must do more than make a game look strange or dreamlike. It should materially shape something central: a world, story, creature, event, mechanic, sound, rule, or structure.

**Dreamcore Horror** is the proposed branch for works shaped by the creator's own frightening dreams, nightmares, or night terrors and designed as horror.

## Four different things people may mean

### 1. Dream aesthetic

A game can look dreamlike through fog, surreal architecture, nostalgia, or impossible spaces. That is a visual style. It does not prove that the creator used a personal dream.

### 2. Dream narrative

A fictional character may enter a dream, remember a dream, or live through a false awakening. Dream-as-story is a theme. It does not by itself establish provenance.

### 3. Dream mechanics

A game may let the player manipulate dream rules, become lucid, or wake into another environment. Dream mechanics are a design choice; they can be inspired by general dream theory or by a personal experience.

### 4. Creator-derived dream work

This is the proposed category's central distinction. The creator can point to their own dream experience and show how it informed a central part of the game. The game might be realistic, abstract, funny, terrifying, or set in another genre entirely.

## Examples already located

Creators have publicly described games based on real dreams, dream journals, recurring dreams, or personal dream imagery. Examples documented in this research include *Clockwork Dreams*, *#21: The World*, *Dream Diary*, *Zemblini: Dream Journal*, *My Magical Dream Journal*, *Archive Equinox*, and the recurring-dream level in *My Nightmares Tell Me to Chase My Dreams*. *OMORI* is a mixed case with substantial dream and nightmare input alongside other influences.

Those examples show that the practice has precedents. They do **not** prove that a new genre has already formed or that any one person invented the idea.

## What Dreamcore is not

- A synonym for any game with a dream theme.
- A synonym for surreal or liminal art.
- A quality award.
- A claim that the creator has a supernatural experience.
- A requirement to disclose trauma or a complete dream.

## Why the name needs care

“Dreamcore” is already used as an internet aesthetic and as the title of a released game. This article uses it descriptively and provisionally. The project does not claim ownership of the word and has not selected a final alternative.
