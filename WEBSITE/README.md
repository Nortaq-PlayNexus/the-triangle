# Future Official Dreamcore Website — Content Architecture

**Status:** unpublished drafts only. Nothing here has been published, registered, or sent to a third party.

## Purpose

A future site could explain the proposed category, preserve evidence, show THE TRIANGLE honestly, and invite voluntary participation. It must not imply that the category is already an industry standard or that existing games have endorsed it.

## Proposed navigation

- `/` — Home
- `/about` — About the initiative
- `/what-is-dreamcore` — Public explanatory article
- `/dreamcore-horror` — Horror branch
- `/specification` — Versioned criteria
- `/the-triangle` — Project page
- `/games` — Candidate/example directory
- `/developers` — Developer information
- `/history` — Evidence-based history
- `/faq` — Frequently asked questions
- `/resources` — Research and definitions
- `/submit` — Voluntary submission form
- `/press-kit` — Facts and assets
- `/contact` — Contact placeholder; no address has been selected

## Content rules

- Put “proposed” or “working category” near the first use of the term.
- Link every factual claim to a source or local evidence ID.
- Distinguish creator statements, project descriptions, platform metadata, and journalism.
- Do not publish private dream details without consent.
- Do not call a tag, search count, or a single example “adoption.”
- Do not use a badge that looks like a platform certification.
- Keep drafts versioned and reversible.
- Do not collect personal information until a privacy and retention policy exists.
