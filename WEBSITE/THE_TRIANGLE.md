# THE TRIANGLE

*Draft project page — not a release announcement.*

## Project description

THE TRIANGLE is a first-person psychological horror game about a sequence of interconnected nightmares, lucid dreaming, recognition, false awakenings, and impossible architecture. The player observes, remembers, recognizes, and tries to wake without being told what the recurring geometric presence is.

## Current development state

- Pre-alpha vertical-slice revamp in progress.
- Unreal Engine 5.8.2 target with a native C++ foundation.
- Childhood bedroom and underground station are procedurally assembled in the current source.
- Lucidity, Recognition, Dream Director, false-awakening manager, anomaly subsystem, audio, save/load, HUD, and core tests are implemented in source.
- Other planned environments, authored assets, full false-bedroom transformation, ending, and current acceptance remain incomplete.
- The documented UE installation gate currently prevents a trustworthy current build/PIE acceptance claim.

## Genre position

THE TRIANGLE is a **provisional Dreamcore Horror candidate** in this research package. Its creator reports a substantial personal night-terror basis, while the repository provides strong evidence for dream-horror mechanics and atmosphere. A public provenance record and completed slice are still needed.

## Privacy

The project does not need to publish a complete account of any nightmare to explain its creative source. Any future public statement should be reviewed and approved by the creator.
