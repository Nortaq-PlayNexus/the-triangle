# Developers — Draft Page

## For creators

If your game is substantially derived from your own dream experiences, you may choose to describe it using the proposed term. Participation is voluntary.

You do not need to disclose:

- the full dream;
- names or locations;
- medical or trauma details;
- a diagnosis;
- private journal entries.

A useful statement can be abstract: “The environments and recurring presence are based on a documented dream pattern,” or “The core rule comes from a recurring false-awakening experience.”

## What a useful submission includes

- game title and developer;
- public link;
- release or version date;
- a short source description;
- which elements are materially derived;
- whether the work is intended as horror;
- permission to quote or paraphrase the statement;
- any wording you want corrected or removed.

## What the directory does not do

- It does not diagnose you.
- It does not require proof of a supernatural event.
- It does not certify quality.
- It does not own your game or your story.
- It does not publish private material without consent.
