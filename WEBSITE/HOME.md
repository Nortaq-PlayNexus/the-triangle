# Draft Home Page — Proposed Dreamcore

> **Draft only. Not published.** This page would introduce a proposed, voluntary category for interactive works derived from creators' own dreams and nightmares.

## What is Dreamcore?

Dreamcore is a proposed game category for works in which a creator's own dream experience is a documented, material source of the game's world, narrative, mechanics, events, entities, sound, or rules.

The word already exists as an internet aesthetic and as the title of a released game. This initiative is not claiming to own it or to replace the existing meaning. It is testing a narrower, provenance-based definition.

## Dreamcore Horror

Dreamcore Horror is the proposed horror branch: a work that meets the base definition and is materially shaped by the creator's own frightening dreams, nightmares, or night terrors.

## THE TRIANGLE

THE TRIANGLE is a first-person psychological dream-horror prototype about interconnected nightmares, lucidity, recognition, false awakenings, and a recurring geometric presence. Its creator reports a personal night-terror basis; the project is still pre-alpha and under evidence review.

## What this site would do

- Explain the proposed definitions.
- Show how games are classified and how uncertainty is handled.
- Preserve source links and access dates.
- Invite creators to participate voluntarily.
- Keep existing games and creators accurately represented.

## Current status

The category is a research proposal, not an established industry standard. No independent adoption is claimed. See the [research audit](../AUDIT/GENRE_INITIATIVE_AUDIT.md) before using promotional language.
