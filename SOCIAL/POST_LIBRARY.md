## Definition posts

### Post 1 — the distinction

A game can look dreamlike without being based on a creator's dream.

The proposed Dreamcore definition asks a stricter question: can the creator identify an own dream experience that materially shaped a central part of the game?

That means source and influence—not just atmosphere.

Research draft; not an established genre.

### Post 2 — four terms

Dream aesthetic: how a game looks.  
Dream narrative: what happens in its story.  
Dream mechanics: how the game plays.  
Creator-derived dream work: what personally documented dream source materially shaped the work.

Only the last is the proposed category's central distinction.

### Post 3 — no private disclosure

A creator should never have to publish a complete nightmare to be taken seriously. “Based on a recurring dream about a train” can be enough for a voluntary, privacy-safe description.

## THE TRIANGLE development posts

### Post 4 — blockout honesty

THE TRIANGLE's current slice is being built in native C++ and procedural BasicShapes before the art pass. The goal is to prove the bedroom → station flow, lucidity, recognition, manifestations, false wakes, persistence, and audio behavior—not to pretend a blockout is final atmosphere.

### Post 5 — the two systems

Lucidity asks: “I know I am dreaming.”  
Recognition asks: “I know what is here.”

Keeping them separate lets THE TRIANGLE make awareness and pattern recognition different player experiences.

### Post 6 — build gate

No public gameplay claim until the Unreal installation is verified and the vertical slice passes an actual run. Documenting the blocker is more useful than hiding it.

## Research posts

### Post 7 — not a priority claim

We found public games whose creators say they are based on real dreams, dream journals, or recurring dreams. That is evidence of a creative practice with precedents—not a reason to manufacture a “first” claim.

### Post 8 — name collision

“Dreamcore” is already used as an internet aesthetic and as a game title. Any category using that word has to explain its definition clearly and avoid implying ownership.

### Post 9 — what counts

The proposed test is simple: if the personal dream source were removed, would a central part of the game change?

If the answer is only “the palette would be different,” the work is aesthetically dreamlike, not confirmed Dreamcore under the proposal.
