# Social Content Strategy — Draft

**Status:** planning only. No account has been created and no post has been scheduled.

## Objective

Build informed awareness around a proposed, evidence-led category without manufacturing consensus or exploiting personal dream material.

## Pillars

1. **THE TRIANGLE development** — honest prototype and engine/build status.
2. **What Dreamcore means** — definition and terminology distinctions.
3. **Dream-to-game development** — how a source becomes a world, mechanic, or event.
4. **UE5 behind the scenes** — code, procedural blockouts, tests, and blockers.
5. **Nightmare reconstruction** — privacy-safe design analysis, never sensational disclosure.
6. **Other developers' work** — accurately sourced examples, clearly labeled as precedents rather than adoption.
7. **Genre history and research** — access dates, uncertainty, and contradictions.
8. **Community submissions** — voluntary, consent-based, moderated.
9. **Short clips/trailers** — only approved, captioned, non-spoiler material.
10. **Developer education** — criteria, privacy, and correction process.

## Content mix

- 40% research/education
- 25% THE TRIANGLE development transparency
- 20% sourced examples and terminology
- 10% community questions
- 5% approved visual clips

This is a planning hypothesis, not a performance target.

## Guardrails

- No “first,” “invented,” “never seen,” or “industry standard” language without new evidence.
- No false adoption, user counts, reviews, votes, or testimonials.
- No private dream content without explicit, specific consent.
- No spam, mass tagging, unsolicited DMs, or cross-community reposting.
- No fabricated accounts, bots, astroturfing, or coordinated review pressure.
- Clearly label drafts, candidates, and provisional classifications.
- Do not imply that a platform, critic, or community endorses the proposal.
- Use content warnings and accessibility captions for horror material.

## Measurement

Measure only approved, useful signals such as substantive feedback, correction requests, documentation downloads, and voluntary submissions. Do not optimize for manufactured virality or raw follower count.

## Approval workflow

1. Draft in `SOCIAL/POST_LIBRARY.md`.
2. Fact-check every external claim.
3. Ask the creator to approve personal source references.
4. Check legal wording and current build status.
5. Publish only after explicit approval.
6. Record the final URL, date, and edits.
