# FAQ for Journalists — Draft

## What is the news?

A developer is documenting a proposed category for games substantially derived from their creators' own dream experiences. The work is being done transparently because the term Dreamcore already has other meanings and the creator does not want to manufacture a genre claim.

## Is THE TRIANGLE the first such game?

No. The research found public works whose creators describe real dreams, dream journals, or recurring dreams. The project does not claim priority.

## Did the creator invent Dreamcore?

No supported claim is made. Dreamcore is already used online as an aesthetic and as the title of a released game.

## Is Dreamcore Horror an established genre?

Not according to the sources reviewed. It is the proposed horror branch of this documentation project.

## What makes a game Dreamcore under the proposal?

It must be interactive, based materially on a documented dream of a credited creator, and reviewable through a source or creator statement. Dreamlike visuals alone do not qualify.

## Is THE TRIANGLE based on the creator's dreams?

The creator's project brief says it is based substantially on personal night-terror experiences. The repository strongly supports the dream-horror design and mechanics, but a public provenance map and current completed slice are still being developed.

## Is the game playable/released?

It is documented as a pre-alpha prototype. A current build/PIE acceptance claim is blocked by the recorded Unreal installation issue. No release is claimed.

## What evidence supports the project?

- Local source and design documents.
- A creator statement in the project brief.
- A Git implementation history beginning 14 September 2026.
- Public creator pages for other personal-dream games.
- Official platform and journalism sources documenting the existing Dreamcore term/title.

## What is uncertain?

- The first origin of the Dreamcore aesthetic.
- The earliest game matching this proposed definition.
- Whether independent creators will adopt the terminology.
- The legal/trademark position of the term.
- Which details of THE TRIANGLE's personal source can be public.

## What are you asking for?

Feedback on whether the definition is clear, useful, respectful of privacy, and distinct from existing dream/nightmare/aesthetic language. The project is not asking for a positive review or an endorsement.
