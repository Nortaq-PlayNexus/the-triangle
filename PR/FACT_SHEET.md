## Premise

The player moves through interconnected nightmares and becomes aware of dream rules while trying to wake. The Triangle is a recurring, ambiguous presence rather than a conventional monster. The player observes, remembers, recognizes, questions reality, and eventually confronts the original nightmare.

## Source basis

The project brief states that THE TRIANGLE is based substantially on the creator's own night-terror/nightmare experiences. The public evidence set currently records this as user-reported provenance, not a fully mapped historical record.
