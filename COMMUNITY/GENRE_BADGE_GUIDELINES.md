# Genre Badge Guidelines

**Status:** design guidance only; no badge has been approved, published, registered, or represented as an official platform mark.

## Purpose

A badge could communicate optional category membership and review status. It must not imply platform ownership, certification, quality, safety, or legal protection.

## Proposed badge states

- `Proposed Dreamcore`
- `Proposed Dreamcore Horror`
- `Candidate — evidence pending`
- `Adjacent — not classified`

The exact visual treatment remains undecided. Any final design should be checked for trademark and accessibility issues.

## Usage requirements

- Use the word “proposed” in nearby text.
- Include a plain-text description.
- Link to the criteria and review date.
- Do not place the badge on a platform that could interpret it as an official certification.
- Do not imply that the creator, publisher, or public endorses the initiative.
- Do not use the badge to rank games or imply quality.
- Do not remove a creator's objection or reuse a withdrawn listing without permission.

## Visual constraints

- Do not imitate Steam, Unreal, PlayStation, Xbox, Epic, itch.io, or another platform's branding.
- Do not copy a platform's badge shape, gradient, iconography, or trade dress.
- Use a distinct, accessible design with sufficient contrast.
- Provide an SVG and text alternative if a final asset is ever approved.
- Avoid imagery that trivializes trauma, nightmares, or mental health.
- Do not use a creator's personal dream image without explicit permission.

## Suggested text

> **Proposed Dreamcore candidate** — This work may be substantially based on the creator's own dream experiences. Classification is voluntary and under review. [Read the criteria.]

## Review record

A badge use should record:

- game/listing ID;
- classification status;
- review date;
- evidence source IDs;
- creator consent;
- version of badge artwork;
- opt-out/withdrawal status.

No badge should be released before Australian IP review and a decision on the colliding “Dreamcore” terminology.
