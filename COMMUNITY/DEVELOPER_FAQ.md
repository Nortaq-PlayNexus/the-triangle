# Developer FAQ

## Do I have to use the term Dreamcore?

No. The proposed directory can record a work for research without asking the creator to use the term publicly.

## Can I join if my game is only dreamlike?

You can submit it as an aesthetic or adjacent example if you wish, but it should not be labeled Dreamcore unless the provenance and materiality criteria are met.

## What if my game is based on a dream journal?

That is a strong fit if the journal belongs to you or a consenting credited creator and its entries materially shape the game.

## What if my source is a recurring nightmare?

It may fit Dreamcore Horror if the game is intended as horror and the source is documented. You can describe it without sharing the nightmare.

## What if several people contributed?

Identify the source-holder and obtain permission from anyone whose personal experience is claimed. A future team rule may be added; until then, reviewers will mark the case for discussion.

## Does the badge give me protection or ownership?

No. A proposed badge would communicate an optional classification, not legal protection or platform endorsement.

## Can I remove my entry?

Yes. A future directory should support correction, anonymity, and withdrawal.

## Will you contact me?

No outreach is currently authorized. A future contact process would require explicit approval and a privacy policy.

## Can I use a pseudonym?

Yes, if the directory's consent and verification process allows it. The maintainer may need enough information to prevent impersonation without publishing private details.

## What if the evidence is incomplete?

The entry can remain `candidate` or `probable`; the directory should not fill the gap with inference.

## What if I disagree with a classification?

Request a source review. The maintainer should show the evidence, explain the rubric, record the disagreement, and allow a safe correction or withdrawal.
