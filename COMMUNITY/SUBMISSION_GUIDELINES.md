# Submission Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0-draft  
**Status:** no submission form is live. These are proposed rules for a future voluntary directory.

## Required fields

1. Game title.
2. Developer or credited creator/team.
3. Public game or project link.
4. Release/version date, or “unknown.”
5. Platform(s).
6. Proposed term, if any.
7. Privacy-safe source description.
8. Material elements derived from the source.
9. Horror intention, if relevant.
10. Evidence links or design-note references.
11. Permission to quote, paraphrase, or list the work.
12. Preferred attribution and withdrawal contact.

## Evidence levels

- **A:** direct creator statement plus dated evidence.
- **B:** direct creator statement with a clear source description.
- **C:** official project copy or attributed interview.
- **D:** third-party/community lead; not enough for confirmation.
- **U:** unresolved or inaccessible.

## Review outcomes

- `confirmed`
- `probable`
- `candidate`
- `adjacent`
- `not_classifiable`
- `withdrawn`

## Privacy rules

- Do not submit a full dream account unless the creator explicitly wants it published.
- Do not request names, addresses, diagnoses, medications, trauma details, or identifying family information.
- Redact or abstract sensitive material before storage.
- Do not infer a mental-health condition from a dream description.
- Allow pseudonym, studio-only attribution, or no public source description.
- Provide a correction and deletion route before accepting submissions.

## What will not be accepted as proof

- A store tag.
- A screenshot or visual resemblance.
- A fan comment.
- A search ranking.
- An AI-generated description with no attributable source.
- A public claim that a game is “based on dreams” when the creator denies or retracts it.

## Moderation

A reviewer must:

- distinguish self-description from third-party description;
- state uncertainty rather than guess;
- avoid copying a creator's wording beyond permission;
- record the date and URL/path of evidence;
- preserve a correction history;
- avoid conflicts of interest and undisclosed promotion.

## Removal

A creator can request removal, anonymity, correction, or a change in classification. Removal should not require a public dispute. The archive may retain a minimal internal record that a listing existed and why it was removed, without retaining sensitive source text.
