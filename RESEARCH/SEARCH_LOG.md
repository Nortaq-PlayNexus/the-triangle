## Direct external sources captured

See `EVIDENCE/EVIDENCE_INDEX.md` and `EVIDENCE/URLS.md` for the source IDs, excerpts, URLs, and access dates. The strongest direct provenance examples in this pass are creator pages for **Clockwork Dreams**, `#21: The World`, `Dream Diary`, `Zemblini: Dream Journal`, `My Magical Dream Journal`, `Archive Equinox`, and the recurring-dream level in `My Nightmares Tell Me to Chase My Dreams`. *OMORI* is a substantial mixed case, and *Lucid Falls* is a direct concept-level case whose finished-game extent is unknown. *LSD: Dream Emulator*, *Neverending Nightmares*, and *Happy Game* are important counterexamples where the documented source is borrowed, personal mental illness, or general/creative influences rather than the creator's own nightmare.

## Search cautions

- A zero-result query is not proof of no game, no term, or no adoption.
- Search results are time-sensitive; the current date is recorded instead of treating them as permanent counts.
- A publisher's use of “dream” in marketing is not a statement that the work is based on the publisher's own dream.
- A fan saying “this feels like a dream game” is not a classification.
- A source can support one proposition and contradict another; both observations are retained.
