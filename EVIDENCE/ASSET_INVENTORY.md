# Evidence Asset Inventory

**Snapshot:** 2026-09-24

## What currently exists

- Human-authored design documents at the project root.
- C++ source and configuration under `Source/` and `Config/`.
- Empty project content directories under `Content/`; no authored `.uasset`, `.umap`, texture, mesh, audio, or UI assets were found.
- Generated output under `Saved/`, `Intermediate/`, `Binaries/`, and `DerivedDataCache/` is not treated as release evidence.
- `Saved/AutoScreenshot.png` is an ignored/generated runtime image. It is not a curated screenshot and does not demonstrate the intended atmosphere or acceptance state.

## Evidence that can be safely added later

With creator approval, the archive could collect:

- a stable repository commit or tag;
- dated design notes;
- a redacted dream-source statement;
- a prototype screenshot or video;
- a trailer or gameplay capture;
- a build hash and platform;
- a dated public announcement;
- a press mention or interview;
- a creator-approved submission record.

## Capture requirements

Every future asset record should include:

- asset ID;
- title and description;
- creator/owner;
- date created and date observed;
- source URL or local path;
- license/permission;
- privacy/consent status;
- hash or version where applicable;
- what historical claim it supports;
- whether it is generated, authored, or third-party.

Do not download or redistribute copyrighted pages, trailers, screenshots, or music without permission. Link to official sources when archiving is not appropriate.
