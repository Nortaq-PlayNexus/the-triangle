# Dreamcore Horror — Overview for Press

## Proposed branch

Dreamcore Horror is a proposed branch for an interactive work that:

1. meets the provenance-and-materiality definition of Dreamcore;
2. is materially shaped by the creator's own frightening dreams, nightmares, night terrors, or comparable dream phenomena; and
3. is intended as horror.

The source and the intended effect must both be present. A dreamlike horror game with no documented personal dream source is not confirmed under the proposal.

## THE TRIANGLE

THE TRIANGLE is a first-person psychological horror prototype about interconnected nightmares, lucid awareness, false awakenings, recurring motifs, and an ambiguous geometric presence. Its creator reports that the project is substantially informed by personal night-terror experiences. The repository provides strong evidence for the dream-horror design and code, but a public provenance map and current completed slice are still needed.

## Privacy

A creator can participate without revealing a complete nightmare. Public descriptions can be abstract, such as “based on a recurring dream involving a train” or “based on a recurring feeling of being watched.”

## Careful language

Dreamcore Horror is a proposal, not an established genre. It overlaps with psychological horror, surreal horror, liminal-space horror, analog horror, nightmare fiction, and autobiographical games. The research does not support “first,” “invented,” or “industry standard” claims.
