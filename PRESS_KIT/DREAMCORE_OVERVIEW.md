# Dreamcore — Overview for Press

## The proposed idea

Dreamcore is a proposed, voluntary category for interactive works in which a creator's own dream experience is a documented, material source of the game's world, narrative, mechanics, events, entities, sound, or rules.

The proposal is intentionally not a synonym for “dreamlike.” A game can use surreal or liminal aesthetics without meeting the provenance test.

## Why the name needs qualification

The research found that “Dreamcore” is already used:

- as a surreal internet aesthetic;
- as the title of a released psychological exploration game;
- in other game titles, assets, music, and community projects.

The project does not claim to own the term and does not recommend trying to monopolise a generic word. It is testing a precise descriptive definition.

## Evidence approach

The initiative separates:

- creator statements;
- official platform descriptions;
- design and repository evidence;
- journalism;
- community/search vocabulary;
- academic research.

It preserves uncertainty and does not count automated tags as adoption.

## Current status

No independent adoption of the proposed definition was verified in the documented research window. The initiative is an invitation to test whether a coherent, voluntary category could emerge.
