## Future asset record format

For every approved asset, record:

- filename/URL;
- description;
- creator/owner;
- capture date;
- source/path;
- license/permission;
- content warnings;
- what claim it supports;
- whether it is current or historical.

Do not redistribute third-party screenshots, cover art, music, or game footage without permission. Link to official sources where appropriate.
