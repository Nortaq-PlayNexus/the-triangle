# THE TRIANGLE — Overview

**Status:** pre-alpha prototype; not a release announcement.

## Premise

*The Triangle* is a first-person psychological horror game about a sequence of interconnected nightmares. The player begins in a familiar but wrong childhood bedroom, follows a door into an underground station, and gradually realizes that apparently separate environments belong to one dream.

The player does not fight a conventional monster. They observe, remember, recognize, question reality, become lucid, manipulate dream rules, and encounter a recurring geometric presence that may be supernatural, psychological, symbolic, or a construct of the dream itself.

## Current systems

- **Lucidity:** event-gated awareness that the player is dreaming.
- **Recognition:** a separate event-gated ability to identify the Triangle's pattern and sense it before seeing it.
- **Dream Director:** presence, manifestations, motifs, recognition, and wake reset.
- **False awakenings:** attempted waking followed by a small wrong detail and continued dream.
- **Environment flow:** procedural childhood bedroom and underground station with a deliberate transition.
- **Audio:** a procedural hum and state-based silence/audio routing.
- **Persistence:** versioned save/load state for progression, environment, door, notebook, and wake depth.

## Development status

The project is in a vertical-slice revamp. The current UE installation is not yet verified according to the project status documents. Other planned environments, authored art, full false-awakening transformations, final ending, and current acceptance are incomplete.

## Genre context

The creator reports a personal night-terror basis. This makes THE TRIANGLE a provisional example for research into the proposed Dreamcore Horror category, not proof of a new or established genre.
