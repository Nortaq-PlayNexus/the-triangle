# Proposed Definition: Dreamcore Horror

**Version:** 1.0-draft  
**Status:** proposed horror branch; not an established formal genre  
**Date:** 2026-09-24

## Short definition

**Dreamcore Horror** is a horror interactive work that satisfies the base [Dreamcore definition](DREAMCORE_DEFINITION.md) and is materially shaped by the creator's own frightening dreams, nightmares, night terrors, or comparable personally experienced frightening dream phenomena.

The category is about **source and intended effect together**:

- **Source:** the creator's own frightening dream experience materially informs the work.
- **Effect:** the work is designed to produce fear, dread, dread of the unknown, psychological pressure, or another clearly identified horror response.

## Mandatory characteristics

1. **Base Dreamcore criteria:** interactive work, personal dream provenance, material influence, traceability, and authorship clarity.
2. **Frightening personal source:** the source is a nightmare, night terror, disturbing dream, or other personally experienced frightening dream phenomenon. A pleasant, neutral, or purely abstract dream does not by itself satisfy the horror branch.
3. **Horror intention:** the creator or publisher presents the work as horror, psychological horror, surreal horror, nightmare horror, or an equivalent experience. The intention must be visible in the work or an attributable description.
4. **Material horror expression:** the personal source shapes one or more central horror elements: environment, entity, event, narrative, mechanic, sound, rule, or progression.

## What kinds of horror may qualify?

- Psychological horror.
- Supernatural or occult horror, provided the creator's dream provenance is documented.
- Existential or liminal horror.
- Slow dread, uncanny horror, and horror without conventional combat.
- Games that transform a nightmare into metaphor rather than reproduce it literally.
- Games that combine a personal dream source with a fictional narrative or mythology.

The category does not require a particular camera angle, enemy type, visual filter, soundtrack, or first-person perspective.

## What does not qualify by itself?

- A dreamcore-looking game with horror lighting but no personal dream source.
- A game inspired by generic nightmares, creepypasta, or the Backrooms.
- A game about mental illness, depression, OCD, or trauma where no dream provenance is documented.
- A game with a nightmare in its title or story but no evidence that the creator's own nightmare shaped it.
- A game that is frightening but whose source is only another author's fiction.
- A jump-scare collection with no personal-source evidence.

## Distinguishing source, theme, and tone

| Question | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| Is the creator's own dream documented as a source? | Provenance |
| Did that source materially shape the game? | Materiality |
| Is the intended experience horror? | Horror branch |
| Does the game look like the current internet “dreamcore” aesthetic? | Visual comparison only |
| Does the story take place in a dream? | Dream theme only |

A game can be dream-themed without being Dreamcore. A game can be Dreamcore without being horror. A game can be Dreamcore Horror without using the internet dreamcore aesthetic.

## Privacy and consent

Night terrors and nightmares can be sensitive. A creator may classify a work using abstracted evidence such as:

- “based on a recurring dream involving a train”;
- “based on a documented false-awakening experience”;
- “based on a recurring feeling of being watched”;

without publishing the full account. Consent, redaction, and the right to withdraw a sensitive description are required for public directory entries.

## Relationship to THE TRIANGLE

THE TRIANGLE's repository documents a first-person psychological horror game about interconnected nightmares, lucidity, recognition, false awakenings, impossible spaces, and a recurring geometric presence. The user's project brief states that it is based substantially on their own night-terror experiences. The repository does not contain a dated source journal or independent public confirmation of that provenance.

Accordingly, THE TRIANGLE is currently a **provisional Dreamcore Horror candidate**, not a confirmed public classification. See `THE_TRIANGLE/DREAMCORE_CLASSIFICATION.md`.

## Version note

“Dreamcore Horror” is retained as a working term because it is the user's requested branch, but its name collides with the broader existing use of “Dreamcore.” Any public launch should explain the proposed definition immediately and should not imply that the phrase is an established platform category.
