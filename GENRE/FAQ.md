# Dreamcore FAQ

**Status:** proposed category FAQ; not an industry standard.  
**Date:** 2026-09-24

## What is Dreamcore?

Under this proposal, Dreamcore is an interactive work substantially derived from a documented dream experience of its creator or credited creator-team member. The source must materially shape the game, not merely create a dreamlike appearance.

## Is Dreamcore already a game genre?

The research does not establish that it is a formal, stable game genre. “Dreamcore” is already used as an internet aesthetic, in community projects, and as the title of a released game. This project is testing a narrower provenance-based definition.

## What is Dreamcore Horror?

It is a proposed horror branch: a work that meets the Dreamcore criteria and is materially shaped by the creator's own frightening dreams, nightmares, night terrors, or comparable experiences, with an attributable horror intention.

## Does a game have to look surreal or liminal?

No. Surrealism, liminal spaces, nostalgia, retro imagery, and first-person perspective are optional. A game can be visually realistic and still be Dreamcore if a personal dream materially shapes its narrative or mechanics.

## Does a game have to be about dreaming?

No. The source can be a dream experience, while the finished game is a fantasy, science-fiction story, comedy, social simulation, or other form. The source relationship must still be traceable.

## Can a game be based on a dream journal?

Yes, if the journal belongs to a credited creator and the entries materially shape the work. A collaborator's diary may be considered under a future team rule, but v1.0 records that case for review rather than assuming it.

## Is a game based on mental illness Dreamcore Horror?

Not automatically. It may be adjacent autobiographical psychological horror. It qualifies only if the creator documents a personal dream/nightmare source and that source materially shapes the work.

## What if the creator does not want to disclose the dream?

That is acceptable. A public entry can use an abstract statement such as “based on a recurring dream about a train” or “based on a recurring feeling of being watched.” No one should be required to publish trauma, names, locations, medical information, or a complete account.

## Can I call my game Dreamcore if I am not sure?

You may self-describe it as “inspired by the proposed Dreamcore concept” or “a candidate for the proposed category.” Do not imply that a platform, reviewer, or existing community has adopted the term.

## Does a tag count as adoption?

No. Tags are discovery data. Adoption requires voluntary public use of the term in a creator or maintainer context, with enough context to distinguish it from a generic dream aesthetic.

## Is THE TRIANGLE the first Dreamcore Horror game?

No responsible basis for that claim exists. The repository describes THE TRIANGLE as a personal-nightmare project, but the research found earlier public works with direct personal-dream statements. THE TRIANGLE's place in the history is provisional.

## Is THE TRIANGLE confirmed Dreamcore Horror?

It is a **provisional candidate**. The user brief supplies a personal-source statement, and the project implements dream/nightmare systems. A dated source mapping, creator confirmation suitable for a public directory, and a functioning current build are still needed.

## Does the badge mean a game is good, safe, or officially endorsed?

No. The proposed badge communicates an optional classification and its review status. It is not a quality award, age rating, legal mark, or platform endorsement.

## Who decides whether a work qualifies?

A voluntary directory maintainer would apply the published rubric, while the creator retains the right to describe or withdraw the work. No central authority is claimed by this proposal.

## Can “Dreamcore Horror” be replaced?

Yes. Alternative names are researched in `ALTERNATIVE_NAMES.md`. The working term is retained only because it is the current project vocabulary; it is not an irrevocable brand decision.

## Where can I read the evidence?

Start with `RESEARCH/GENRE_LANDSCAPE.md`, `EVIDENCE/EVIDENCE_INDEX.md`, and `AUDIT/GENRE_INITIATIVE_AUDIT.md`. Every classification should link back to a source and an access date.
